# SEO and AI-search delivery lifecycle

Use this lifecycle when the outcome is a site change or recurring operating program, not merely one
bounded audit or report. The domain caller owns the outcome and every owner decision.

## Ownership

- **The `seo` skill owns the method.** It supplies the source-backed checks, four-gate order, SEO
  quality scorecard, red-flag handling, and planning rubric. It is not a role or a separate planner.
- **The domain caller owns planning.** Milo, Magenta, or another authorized domain owner applies the
  method to complete specialist returns, ranks options, recommends direction, and keeps the decision
  pending for the named owner.
- **Beacon owns evidence.** Beacon retrieves the baseline, traffic-quality and disposition evidence,
  page and technical evidence, and red flags. It states what is established or missing without
  ranking, recommending, issuing a verdict, or deciding.
- **`managing-marketing-work` owns orchestration.** It sequences specialist evidence, domain-owned
  planning, approval, implementation, independent verification, observation, and readout. It does
  not create another strategy owner.

A shared method does not merge these roles. Holding `seo` lets Beacon apply its audit and evidence
rules and lets the domain caller apply its planning rules; each actor remains bound by its own role.

## 1. Frame the decision

Name the property, audience, business outcome, approved direction, scope, authority, reporting
window, and completion proof. Separate an evidence request from a request to change the site.

## 2. Establish traceable evidence

- Beacon retrieves authorized property, page, performance, analytics, and supplied-file evidence and
  returns source trails, definitions, comparable windows, calculations, and limits.
- Build the measurement baseline before growth planning: Search Console impressions and clicks;
  organic landing behavior; leads or purchases; and available working, qualified, disqualified,
  converted, and unconverted dispositions. If the named business outcome or required disposition
  path is missing, measurement repair is the first planned outcome.
- Scout establishes published market, customer, competitor-strategy, or category claims when those
  claims affect the decision.
- A supplied crawler-log extract is evidence only after provenance, coverage period, parser,
  crawler-identity verification, exclusions, and row-count reconciliation are recorded. A user-agent
  string alone does not prove crawler identity.
- Bing Webmaster Tools AI Performance evidence is a dated UI or supplied-export observation unless
  Microsoft publishes a supported API for that report. Do not relabel ordinary Bing search metrics
  as citation evidence.
- Query demand for a term the property does not rank for requires a named demand source. Search
  Console absence is not zero demand; an estimate is not observed business value.
- Search Console bulk-export data is a supplied BigQuery result until the caller holds separately
  authorized warehouse access. Record the export configuration date because it is not retroactive.

If a required source cannot be reached, continue with unaffected evidence and mark the dependent
ranking provisional.

## 3. Rank and recommend

The domain caller applies the SEO planning method to the decision, approved direction, and complete
specialist returns. It follows the four gates in order: measurement readiness and baseline,
technical quality and red-flag resolution, growth planning, then content expansion. It separates
foundational defects, evidence-supported enhancements, and expansion; ranks options by impact,
confidence, effort, risk, dependencies, and time to evidence; and proposes the smallest useful test
and measurement contract. It does not retrieve missing specialist evidence while ranking or convert
its recommendation into approval. A critical security, manual-action, availability, blocking, or
conversion-path red flag interrupts the sequence for containment; any other red flag must clear the
technical gate before growth or expansion.

## 4. Record the owner decision

The domain caller presents the recommendation, alternatives, uncertainty, and explicit approval
needed. Only the named owner can select direction, accept risk, authorize implementation, or change
the approved phase plan. Preserve rejected and deferred options with their evidence date.

## 5. Brief and implement

After approval, the domain caller may turn the selected option into an implementation brief with
acceptance, guardrails, release verification, observation window, readout date, and stop conditions. Route copy,
design, development, analytics instrumentation, or configuration to its actual owner. The plan is
not authority to publish, deploy, configure, contact, or spend.

## 6. Independently verify

Use an artifact-qualified reviewer who did not produce the finished artifact. Technical code review
establishes implementation quality; it does not certify SEO evidence. Beacon independently verifies
the affected production surface, measurement behavior, and source trail after an authorized release.
The domain caller may check the implementation against its approved brief but cannot substitute for
Beacon's independent evidence verification.

## 7. Observe and read out

Wait for the predeclared observation window and source latency. Beacon returns comparable acquisition,
business-outcome, crawl/index, performance, and guardrail evidence. The domain caller compares the
observed result with the approved measurement contract and recommends keep, iterate, stop, or gather
more evidence; the owner decides. Report unavailable or immature evidence as such rather than zero.

## 8. Operate the cadence

Run evidence reporting before weekly planning. A report may show a proposed next-week focus, but it
must label that focus pending until the planning step and owner decision occur. Preserve dated source
artifacts, definitions, approved phase direction, release proof, and readout dates so later reports do
not rewrite history.
