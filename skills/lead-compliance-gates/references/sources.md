# Sources

Verified August 12, 2026. Prefer dated primary sources for live decisions. eCFR is authoritative but unofficial
and can lag; confirm material rules against official publications and later orders. This list supports the skill's
framework and is not an exhaustive legal survey.

## Federal statutes, regulations, and official codification

- [47 U.S.C. § 227](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=%28title%3A47+section%3A227%28a%29+edition%3Aprelim%29)
  — TCPA definitions, prohibited calls, FCC authority, and private remedies; House Office of the Law Revision
  Counsel text checked through July 14, 2026.
- [47 C.F.R. § 64.1200, dated August 10, 2026](https://www.ecfr.gov/on/2026-08-10/title-47/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-64/subpart-L/section-64.1200)
  — current FCC consent, PEWC, artificial/prerecorded message, DNC, revocation, confirmation, definitions, and
  reassigned-number provisions. Read paragraph (a)(10) with the current waiver below.
- [47 C.F.R. § 64.1601(e), dated August 10, 2026](https://www.ecfr.gov/on/2026-08-10/title-47/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-64/subpart-P/section-64.1601)
  — telemarketing caller-ID transmission and blocking rules.
- [47 C.F.R. § 64.1604, dated August 10, 2026](https://www.ecfr.gov/on/2026-08-10/title-47/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-64/subpart-P/section-64.1604)
  — misleading/inaccurate caller ID with prohibited intent.
- [16 C.F.R. § 310.2](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-310/section-310.2)
  — current TSR definitions, including seller, telemarketer, telemarketing, and EBR.
- [16 C.F.R. § 310.3](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-310/section-310.3)
  — deceptive practices and assisting/facilitating liability.
- [16 C.F.R. § 310.4](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-310/section-310.4)
  — National/entity DNC, prerecorded calls, abandonment, hours, disclosures, caller ID, and safe harbors.
- [16 C.F.R. § 310.5](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-310/section-310.5)
  — current five-year records, call details, consent, DNC, Registry, contracts, and records safe harbor.
- [16 C.F.R. § 310.6](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-310/section-310.6)
  — exemptions and the current limited B2B treatment.
- [16 C.F.R. § 310.8](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-310/section-310.8)
  — National DNC access, subscription, SAN, and annually changing fees. Do not hard-code fee amounts.
- [18 U.S.C. § 2511(2)(d)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/18/2511)
  — federal one-party interception rule and limitation; state recording rules remain independent.

## FCC orders, court decisions, and current status

- [Facebook, Inc. v. Duguid, 592 U.S. 395 (2021)](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/20pdf/592us2r23_1bn2.pdf)
  — federal ATDS definition. It does not decide artificial/prerecorded voice, DNC, or state law.
- [Insurance Marketing Coalition v. FCC, 127 F.4th 303 (11th Cir. 2025)](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DOC-409525A1.pdf)
  — vacated Part III.D of FCC 23-107, including the categorical one-to-one and logical/topical additions.
- [FCC DA 25-621](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DA-25-621A1.pdf) and
  [90 Fed. Reg. 42137](https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/FR-2025-08-29/2025-16641)
  — restored the pre-2023 PEWC rule text effective August 29, 2025.
- [FCC 23-107](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/FCC-23-107A1.pdf)
  — marketing-text DNC treatment survives; do not rely on its vacated Part III.D.
- [FCC 24-24](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/FCC-24-24A1.pdf) and
  [89 Fed. Reg. 82518](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2024-10-11/pdf/2024-23605.pdf)

- [FCC DA 26-12](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DA-26-12A1.pdf)
  — partial cross-category revocation waiver through January 31, 2027. High volatility; check for later action.
- [FCC 25-76](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/FCC-25-76A1.pdf)
  — open proposals on caller ID and revocation scope, not current law.
- [FCC 24-17](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/FCC-24-17A1.pdf)
  — AI-generated/cloned voices are artificial voice under the TCPA rules.
- [FCC 24-84](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/FCC-24-84A1.pdf)
  — proposed AI-use disclosure. A proposal is not current FCC law.
- [FCC 18-177](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/FCC-18-177A1.pdf) and the official contractor's
  [Reassigned Numbers Database overview](https://www.reassigned.us/about)
  — database and narrow safe-harbor foundation; current elements are in § 64.1200(m).
- [FCC 13-54](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/FCC-13-54A1.pdf)

- [FCC 12-21](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/FCC-12-21A1_Rcd.pdf)
  — PEWC and no-purchase-condition foundation; use current CFR wording for live decisions.

## FTC rulemaking and operational guidance

- [2024 TSR final rule, 89 Fed. Reg. 26760](https://www.federalregister.gov/d/2024-07180)
  — five-year records, call-detail requirements, consent details, voice-clone/soundboard treatment, effective dates,
  and B2B misrepresentation expansion.
- [FTC National DNC Q&A for sellers and telemarketers](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/qa-telemarketers-sellers-about-dnc-provisions-tsr-0)
  — operational seller/SAN, subscription, EBR, permission, and vendor guidance. It is staff guidance; verify rules
  against the CFR.
- [FTC TSR compliance guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-telemarketing-sales-rule)
  — operational explanation, including seller-direct prerecorded consent and vendor duties. Its two-year
  recordkeeping and broad B2B passages are stale; use current §§ 310.5 and 310.6 instead.
- [FTC CAN-SPAM compliance guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business)
  — commercial email, headers, subjects, ad identity, postal address, opt-out, 10-business-day completion,
  suppression sharing, and vendor monitoring.

### Source map for the consent-evidence example

The bad `consent=true` plus certificate-URL example in `SKILL.md` maps to the consent-farm and hidden-recipient
failures illustrated by Operation Stop Scam Calls and the Response Tree matter above. A third-party certificate
does not replace the underlying proof.

The good example's seller, number, channel/technology, no-purchase-condition presentation, signature event,
and exact disclosure map to current [47 C.F.R. § 64.1200](https://www.ecfr.gov/on/2026-08-10/title-47/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-64/subpart-L/section-64.1200).
Its exact request, purpose, consent copy, consumer/number, timestamp, and source lineage map to current
[16 C.F.R. § 310.5](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-310/section-310.5).
Preserving the render and hash is an engineering evidence strategy supported by the enforcement failures, not
a claim that those exact storage fields are universally mandated.

## Volatility and coverage limits

- The checked FCC revocation waiver expires January 31, 2027 unless changed.
- State solicitation, privacy, data-broker, carrier, insurance, and health-data requirements are high-volatility.
- Equipment “capacity,” affiliate expectations, jurisdiction, exemptions, seller identity, consent sufficiency,
  private rights, and limitation periods can be fact- and court-specific.
- No exhaustive lower-court citator, 50-state table, carrier-provider survey, or every vertical regime is included.
- Re-run primary-source research and obtain counsel approval before using a rule profile for production traffic.
