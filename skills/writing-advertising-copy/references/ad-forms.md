# Write for advertising forms

Platform fields and limits change. Verify the current first-party specification or supplied account
UI before finalizing counts. This reference defines the writing behavior, not campaign setup.

## Responsive search ads

Write a modular asset system rather than one linear ad. Supply the requested headline,
description, path, business-name, sitelink, callout, or other copy fields within verified limits.
Unless a position is intentionally pinned by an authorized requirement, assume assets can appear in
different orders and combinations.

Assign diverse roles across assets:

- query or category recognition;
- product or offer identity;
- supported benefit or capability;
- differentiator or proof;
- exact price, promotion, location, availability, or term;
- objection or uncertainty reduction; and
- approved action.

Each asset must stand alone truthfully and combine without repetition, contradiction, broken
grammar, or a qualification becoming detached from its claim. Do not chase an `Ad Strength` rating
with redundant or unsupported assets; it is platform feedback about the supplied asset system, not
proof of conversion, rank, auction success, landing-page quality, or offer quality.

## Paid social feed, carousel, Story, and Reel ads

Separate primary text, headline, description, on-creative or frame text, spoken script, cover, CTA
label, disclosure, and accessibility requirements. The creative and first visible words establish
recognition; supporting text explains value or proof; the CTA names the available action.

Adapt to the paid placement without imitating organic language that does not fit the brand. Keep key
message and material disclosure available in the creative sequence when a collapsed caption or
sound-off view would hide them. Never invent an unseen asset; write production requirements and
defer visual-dependent alt text or transcripts.

## Sponsored or promoted Pins

Write title, description, on-Pin text, CTA where available, and destination as one promise. Use
natural approved topic or query language so the Pin is intelligible in discovery. The promoted
product, offer, visual, keywords, and landing page must agree. Do not turn a Pin into a keyword list
or promise a guide, result, price, or promotion the destination does not provide.

## Display, native, and other paid units

Identify which fields may show together or separately. Give the visual one clear communication job
and let headlines and descriptions add the offer, value, proof, or action. Avoid vague copy that
could advertise anything. Sponsored or native placements must remain identifiable as advertising;
voice fit does not justify disguising commercial intent.

For any unlisted platform, request or verify its current asset schema, combination behavior,
truncation, safe zones, disclosure rules, and destination requirements. Return copy only. Do not
create the campaign, select audiences, set bids or budget, upload assets, or launch.
