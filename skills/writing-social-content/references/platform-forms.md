# Write for social platform forms

Platform features change. Use these as durable content distinctions, then verify any field, limit,
format, or product behavior that materially affects the deliverable from current first-party
guidance.

## Instagram

### Feed image or photo set

Treat the supplied visual as the first sentence. The caption can identify the point, add story or
context, substantiate a product fact, credit relevant people, disclose a relationship, and offer an
approved next step. It should not narrate every visible detail or claim a detail that was not
supplied. Provide separate alt text or an alt-text requirement when requested or needed.

### Carousel

Give every slide one job in a coherent sequence. Common jobs include promise, context, steps,
evidence, example, limitation, recap, and next action, but no sequence is mandatory. The first slide
must name or frame the value truthfully; later slides must deliver it. Keep essential qualifiers on
the relevant slide. Return slide-by-slide on-creative copy plus a caption that adds context rather
than restating the deck.

### Reel

Separate spoken script, on-screen text, cover text, caption, and accessibility needs. Make the idea
intelligible without assuming trending audio or a specific shot. Keep important on-screen copy
readable and timed to the relevant claim. Request accurate captions or a transcript for speech;
never write a transcript for unseen or unheard media.

### Story

Write frame-by-frame with the interaction or destination each frame actually supports. A poll,
question, link, mention, or sticker is a production requirement unless its availability and target
are supplied. Keep critical context and disclosure in the sequence rather than assuming viewers saw
an earlier feed post.

Instagram's first-party Best Practices area gives creators evolving and personalized creation,
engagement, reach, monetization, and guidelines advice. Do not hard-code universal posting
frequency, caption length, Reel length, hashtag count, or trend use as writing truth. When the owner
asks for optimization, use their current account guidance and results as evidence.

## Pinterest

A Pin is a visual bookmark that can be encountered through search, recommendations, boards, and
saves. Write for a person evaluating whether the idea is relevant now or worth returning to later.

- **Title:** name the specific idea, object, or benefit accurately, with important meaning early.
- **Description:** add useful context and natural subject terms that match the creative and destination.
- **On-Pin text:** make the visual's promise legible and specific without stuffing or duplicating metadata.
- **Destination:** align the title, description, creative, and link; never promise a guide, product,
  template, or result the destination does not provide.
- **Board or topic context:** suggest only when the brief supplies or authorizes it; do not invent a
  board, topic tag, product tag, or link.

Distinguish evergreen usefulness from a genuinely timely moment. Avoid duplicate copy presented as
fresh creative, keyword lists, clickbait, and descriptions unrelated to the destination. Article
Pins may draw title, description, and author information from the linked article; keep that metadata
consistent with the source rather than creating a stronger claim for Pinterest.
Words such as `why`, `how`, `results`, `lessons`, `guide`, and `what happened` are destination
promises, not harmless headline decoration. Use them only when the supplied source actually
delivers that material.

## Other platforms

Do not force an Instagram or Pinterest template onto another platform. Ask for or verify the
surface's current fields and constraints, then apply the same audience, content-object, truth,
voice, accessibility, and no-publication controls. Separate organic content from paid advertising:
an organic-copy request does not authorize campaign strategy, spend, targeting, or ad claims.
