# Write social content

Write for a particular audience moment, content object, and platform surface. Social copy is not a
long article cut short, the same caption pasted everywhere, or an excuse to imitate trends without
the author's voice and product truth.

## Establish the social brief

Record:

```text
Platform, surface, and organic/paid status:
Account, owner, and speaking voice:
Audience, relationship to the account, and moment of encounter:
Content object: supplied image, video, carousel, article, product fact, event, or idea:
What is being promoted, shared, or made useful:
Audience need, desire, question, objection, or identity supported by evidence:
Relevant value, differentiator, and proof:
Approved objective, monetization path, primary success metric, and useful response:
Search intent, season or planning moment, and evidence:
One message and supporting facts:
Approved action or destination:
Voice, tone, English variant, and examples:
Required fields, accessibility needs, limits, and variants:
Claim, disclosure, approval, and publication boundaries:
```

Ask for a missing content object or describe the copy as conditional; never invent what an unseen
image, clip, product, customer, author, or event depicts. Distinguish drafting from posting. A
request to write a post authorizes returning copy, not accessing an account, uploading, scheduling,
publishing, messaging, or replying.

A request whose only action is to publish or schedule already completed copy is a near miss: do not
load this writing workflow to operate or police the account action. When creation, adaptation, or
review is also requested, perform only that content work and keep the external effect separate.

Read [copy and voice](references/copy-and-voice.md) when shaping the message, modeling an author or
brand, adapting one idea across platforms, or reviewing quality and truth. Read [platform forms](references/platform-forms.md)
for Instagram, Pinterest, or another named surface. Read the [Pinterest content playbook](references/pinterest.md)
for any Pinterest portfolio, traffic, save, follower, timing, format, product-content, or
monetization request.

When the requested artifact is paid or sponsored ad copy, use the advertising-copy workflow
instead. An organic product post remains social content even when it promotes something; payment,
an ad unit, or a search-ad asset changes the writing contract.

Content planning does not authorize selecting whether the business should invest in Pinterest,
choosing a commercial model, setting spend, or claiming an outcome. Require the owner-approved
objective and monetization path, or return the available content paths and the decision still
needed. Turn that direction into a portfolio, Pin briefs, and a measurement handoff; leave account
analysis to its evidence owner.

## Choose the content job

State the job before drafting: orient, explain, teach, announce, demonstrate, invite, entertain,
reflect, promote, or lead to a relevant next step. Reduce the source to one defensible message plus
the minimum context needed to understand it. Make the audience recognize why this belongs in their
moment through a supported need, desire, tension, identity, or useful outcome. The opening,
on-creative text, caption, and call to action must work together rather than compete or repeat.

Build a small evidence ledger for product, customer, performance, comparative, quoted, or timely
claims. Use supplied approved facts and primary sources where available. Do not invent a launch
state, result, urgency, testimonial, quotation, personal experience, visual detail, promotion,
deadline, link, hashtag, handle, or next step. Disclosures and regulated wording remain attached to
the claim they qualify.

A feature name or behavior does not establish its motivation, intended benefit, audience problem,
or outcome. Labeling an inference as `the aim`, `designed to`, or `intended to` does not make it
approved product direction. Request that direction or omit it. A familiar domain observation is
also a claim; do not add generic setup merely because it sounds plausible.

Attraction is earned relevance, not maximum excitement. Lead with the most specific supported
connection between this audience and what is being promoted: a useful result, recognized problem,
credible difference, revealing detail, or timely reason. Preserve the approved voice while making
that value easy to recognize. Do not flatten it into generic promotional language.

## Draft for the surface

Return only relevant fields, labeled clearly. A complete content package may include:

```text
Platform / surface
Audience moment and content job
Primary copy
On-creative or frame-by-frame text
Title, description, or link text where the surface uses them
Approved call to action
Accessibility copy or production requirements
Claim, source, disclosure, and approval notes
Meaningfully distinct variants, when requested
Content portfolio, cadence, and measurement handoff, when planning was requested
```

Keep on-creative words legible and purposeful. Let the caption add context, story, evidence, or a
next step the creative cannot carry. Do not bury necessary context or a disclosure in hashtag
blocks. Use hashtags, keywords, emoji, line breaks, prompts, or trends only when they serve the
brief and current platform convention; no fixed count or tactic is universally optimal.

For a cross-platform request, adapt the audience moment, surface, information order, and action.
Preserve the same facts and recognizable voice, but do not merely truncate or relabel one draft.
When a platform's current field, length, or product behavior matters, verify it from first-party
guidance or use a supplied current specification rather than relying on memory.

## Review before return

Check in this order:

1. **Truth and authority:** content object, claims, quotation, state, disclosure, approved action.
2. **Audience value:** one useful message for the actual follower, viewer, searcher, or saver.
3. **Platform fit:** correct fields, sequence, visual-copy relationship, discovery context, and action.
4. **Voice:** approved author or brand patterns adapted to the moment without copied catchphrases.
5. **Language:** clarity, grammar, rhythm, English variant, names, links, and scannability.
6. **Accessibility:** meaning beyond visuals, useful text alternatives, readable hashtags and emoji,
   captions or transcripts where needed, and no essential information conveyed only by style.
7. **Boundary:** return drafts and requirements as text; do not post, schedule, upload, or engage.

On the truth pass, compare every title, hook, question, implication, and call to action against the
source—not only declarative sentences. A question can still promise an answer the source does not
contain. Audit every past, ongoing, and future verb. A prototype, test, draft, or result does not
authorize `we are continuing`, `another round`, `next we will`, `coming soon`, or a promise to share
more. Use only an approved next step; otherwise stop at the established state.
